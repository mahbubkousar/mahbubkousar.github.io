ResumeSync

An ATS optimizer, also known as a mirror with a scoring engine.

AI Product Resume Optimization Web Platform

ResumeSync is an ATS resume optimization platform that helps users compare resume content against target job descriptions. It combines structured scoring with AI-assisted manual and conversational editing, because apparently resumes were not stressful enough without real-time metrics.

Backend
Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Redis-cached analytics.
AI Features
LLM-powered keyword extraction and conversational editing support.
Output
Automated PDF export and real-time ATS-style score feedback.

Scoring Engine

The scoring engine benchmarks resume content against target job descriptions, surfacing missing keywords, content gaps, and formatting issues. The goal is to make optimization explicit without turning the user into a passive passenger inside an opaque AI rewrite machine.

Editing Workflow

ResumeSync supports both manual edits and AI-assisted conversational editing. This lets users preserve voice and intent while still benefiting from keyword extraction and targeted improvement suggestions.

System Notes

Redis-cached analytics reduce repeated computation, while PostgreSQL stores structured resume and job data. Automated PDF export turns the optimized draft into a usable artifact, completing the sacred software loop: input, anxiety, output.
