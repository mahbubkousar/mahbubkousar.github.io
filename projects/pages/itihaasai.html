ItihaasAI

A historical archive that decided plain storage was insufficiently dramatic.

CSE499 Capstone Full-Stack AI Bengali/English

ItihaasAI is a full-stack AI historical archive for linking events, people, locations, and documents into a searchable knowledge system. Its central hypothesis is that historical material becomes more useful when it can be queried, verified, visualized, and politely interrogated by a bilingual assistant.

Stack
React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Python FastAPI.
Security
JWT authentication and role-based access control.
AI Layer
OCR, fuzzy matching, vector retrieval, NER, credibility scoring, and GraphRAG.

Principal Construction

The system models historical knowledge as a graph, connecting source documents to people, places, and events. Full-text search handles direct lookup, while graph visualization gives the archive a navigable structure that is more useful than a pile of PDFs wearing a database costume.

Verification Pipeline

A six-layer fact-verification pipeline combines OCR, fuzzy matching, vector retrieval, named entity recognition, and credibility scoring. The goal is to surface relevant evidence while flagging source inconsistencies and potentially tampered material.

Assistant Interface

A GraphRAG-powered Bengali/English voice assistant answers historical questions with citation-grounded responses. This gives the archive a conversational access layer while keeping the evidence trail visible, because unsupported historical confidence is how arguments start at family gatherings.
